## Q1. Which 10 products lead by usage, ranked, with dated sources?

### Takeaway
ChatGPT leads on every measure. It has more than 1B monthly users and roughly 55-57% of chatbot web visits. Meta AI (by embedded reach) and Gemini (950M MAU) come next. Going strictly by reported monthly users, positions 4-10 are Doubao, Claude, Qwen, Copilot, DeepSeek, Grok and Perplexity. Mistral Le Chat has no published user figure anywhere near that scale, so it falls outside the top 10. The 10th slot goes to the China-only apps Doubao and Qwen, not to Character.ai, Manus or Genspark.

## Q2. For each product: free and paid tiers, prices, how limits work, family plans, team plans, extra-usage purchases

### Takeaway
The market has settled on about $20/mo for the main paid plan, a $100-$200+ power tier, and a cheap entry tier at $5-$8 (ChatGPT Go, Google AI Plus, Meta One Plus). Team seats run about $20-$40 per user per month, and several products now have Standard and Premium seat types. **Google is the only major product whose AI plans can be shared with a family (up to 5 others).** Microsoft 365 Family shares the Office apps but keeps the AI for the owner, and OpenAI has no family plan. Limits are stated as multiples of a lower tier rather than message counts. Claude is the product that publishes rolling 5-hour plus weekly windows and sells overflow at API rates.

### Cited Findings

**ChatGPT**
- Plans: Free; Go $8/mo (more messages, uploads and images; may show ads); Plus $20; Pro in three tiers at $100 / $200 / $500. Pro $100 = 5x Plus limits and 5x Plus Codex. Pro $200 = 20x Plus, 1M context, unlimited Sora. Pro $500 reportedly 25x Plus plus "Ultrafast". Older sources list only $100 and $200 — [NoCode MBA](https://www.nocode.mba/articles/chatgpt-pricing); [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chatgpt-plans-comparison); [The AI Career Lab, Nov 2026](https://theaicareerlab.com/blog/chatgpt-pricing-plans-explained)
- Free tier: everyday text chat reportedly became unlimited on Free and Go in Aug 2026, subject to abuse guardrails. Uploads, image creation, voice, deep research and memory stay capped, and the context window is ~27K tokens. Earlier caps (about 10 messages per 5 h, or 25 per 5 h on a mini model) are likely out of date. This is not confirmed on OpenAI's own page — [Tokn.watch](https://tokn.watch/blog/does-chatgpt-have-limits/); [FreeAINews](https://freeainews.com/news/chatgpt-pricing-changes-2026/); [ai-toolbox](https://www.ai-toolbox.co/chatgpt-management-and-productivity/chatgpt-limits-messages-tokens-rate-2026)
- Business: per seat with a 2-seat minimum. Standard $25/seat/mo, or $20 billed annually. Premium $125/mo, or $100 annually, at 5x Standard usage — [NoCode MBA](https://www.nocode.mba/articles/chatgpt-pricing); [CloudZero](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/how-much-does-chatgpt-cost/)
- Family: none as of Nov 2026; every consumer subscription covers one person — [izzedo](https://www.izzedo.chat/blog/ai-family-plans); [The AI Career Lab](https://theaicareerlab.com/blog/chatgpt-pricing-plans-explained)

**Google Gemini (Google AI plans via Google One)**
- AI Plus: $4.99 in most sources, $7.99 in others (TechCabal Jun 2026; Digital Applied May 2026), and Google's own pages reportedly differ. AI Pro: $19.99. AI Ultra: two tiers, ~$99.99 (20 TB storage, 5x Pro usage) and ~$199.99 (30 TB, 20x Pro). The plans were restructured after I/O in May 2026 — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/06/05/google-ai-subscription-plans-explained/); [NoCode MBA](https://www.nocode.mba/articles/gemini-pricing); [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/google-ai-plans-free-plus-pro-ultra-2026)
- Limits are multiples of a lower tier: Plus 2x Free, Pro 4x Free, Ultra 5x or 20x Pro. No fixed message caps are published in these sources — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/06/05/google-ai-subscription-plans-explained/); [TheDroidGuy](https://thedroidguy.com/google-ai-plus-vs-pro-vs-ultra-whats-the-difference-besides-usage-allowance-1272353)
- Family: Plus, Pro and Ultra all include "family sharing with up to five others". The manager pays, each member uses their own Google Account, and members get the AI benefits at no extra cost. Some guides say only "select" benefits are shared — [Krater](https://krater.ai/blog/gemini-family-plan); [AIonX](https://aionx.co/gemini-advanced-reviews/google-ai-pro-family-sharing/); [izzedo](https://www.izzedo.chat/blog/ai-family-plans)

**Meta AI**
- The base product is free inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and the Meta AI app. Paid AI plans were first tested in Singapore, Guatemala and Bolivia: Meta One Plus $7.99/mo and Meta One Premium $19.99/mo (CNBC, 27 May 2026). Meta One reportedly launched 15 Sep 2026 with tiers from $2.99 to $499/mo. One report gives Plus at $14.99, which conflicts with the rest — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/27/meta-testing-ai-subscription-services-cheapest-plan-at-7point99-a-month.html); [TechCrunch, 15 Sep 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/meta-expands-subscription-push-with-new-ai-focused-plans/); [TechCrunch, 27 May 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/meta-officially-launches-instagram-facebook-and-whatsapp-subscriptions-with-more-to-come-including-ai-plans/); [ExplainX](https://explainx.ai/blog/meta-one-subscription-tiers-ai-features-2026)

**Doubao (ByteDance)**
- Free chat stays free. Paid tiers launched in mainland China (~24 Jun 2026): Standard ¥68 (~$9.40)/mo, Enhanced ¥200 (~$27.70), Professional ¥500 (~$69.20), up to ¥5,088/yr. The paid features focus on presentation generation, data analysis and video — [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/06/25/chinas-top-ai-app-doubao-goes-paid-ending-free-service-race); [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/06/02/bytedances-doubao-to-launch-paid-plans-in-late-june-link-with-douyin-e-commerce-push/); [Caixin](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-05-05/bytedance-plans-subscriptions-for-ai-chatbot-doubao-102440708.html); [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/0yeEiJ4BNl__-4_Gv41K)

**Claude (Anthropic)**
- Pro $20/mo ($17/mo billed yearly), at least 5x Free per session. Max 5x is $100. Max 20x is $200, and several sources say it was renamed "Ultra" (one says from 1 Nov 2026) with no 5-hour limit for verified accounts and a weekly allowance of 10x Pro. This is disputed, so confirm on claude.com/pricing — [whizi](https://whizi.io/resources/claude-pricing/); [ai-toolbox](https://www.ai-toolbox.co/claude-models/claude-pricing-plans-2026); [Runelab](https://runelab.ai/en/blog/post/claude-pricing-pro-max-ultra-team); [Tandem, Oct 2026](https://www.wearetandem.ai/en/blog/tools/claude-max-pro-pricing-plans)
- How limits work: a rolling 5-hour session limit plus a weekly limit on Pro, Max and Team. Five-hour limits were reportedly doubled on 6 May 2026 and peak-hour reductions removed. Usage past the limit is bought as usage credits at API rates. The top model is credits-only on Pro, and on Max/Ultra and Premium seats it is included up to half the weekly limit — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/claude-usage-limits); [MorphLLM](https://www.morphllm.com/claude-code-usage-limits); [EnrichLabs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/claude-pricing)
- Team: Standard seat $25/mo ($20 annual) at ~1.25x Pro per session; Premium seat $125/mo ($100 annual) at ~6.25x Pro. Enterprise from ~$20/seat plus usage at API rates. Sources disagree on whether the monthly Standard price is $20 or $25 — [Layer3Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/claude-pro-vs-max-for-teams); [whizi](https://whizi.io/resources/claude-pricing/)
- Family: no family plan found — [izzedo](https://www.izzedo.chat/blog/ai-family-plans)

**Qwen (Alibaba)**
- No pricing found in this research (see Gaps).

**Microsoft Copilot**
- Consumer: free Copilot. Microsoft 365 Premium $19.99/mo (~$199.99/yr) is an individual plan with Copilot for the owner only. Microsoft 365 Family covers up to 6 people (the price snippet was ambiguous, either $129.99 or $199.99/yr), but its AI features are reported as owner-only — [aiviewer](https://aiviewer.ai/guides/ai-pricing-comparison-2026/); [noxi](https://noxi.ai/blog/best-ai-subscription)
- Business: Copilot Business $21/user/mo list, $18 first-year promotion for purchases from 1 Jul to 31 Dec 2026, on top of an existing Microsoft 365 Business plan — [AI Pricing Index, Nov 2026](https://aibusinessweekly.net/p/ai-pricing-index); [nhimg](https://nhimg.org/enterprise-ai-assistant-plans-compared)

**DeepSeek**
- The consumer chat app and web are free. No consumer subscription was found in this research; paid use is the API only — [Second Talent](https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/deepseek-statistics/); [AI Business Weekly](https://aibusinessweekly.net/p/deepseek-statistics-2026) (free status reported, not confirmed on DeepSeek's own page)

**Grok (xAI)**
- SuperGrok $30/mo; SuperGrok Plus reportedly $100/mo. One unverified source says new customers have been put on "The X Subscription" since 22 Oct 2026. Grok Business is $30/user/mo — [aiviewer](https://aiviewer.ai/guides/ai-pricing-comparison-2026/); [perspectiveai](https://perspectiveai.xyz/ai-pricing-guide-2026-every-plan-compared/); [nhimg](https://nhimg.org/enterprise-ai-assistant-plans-compared)

**Perplexity**
- Pro $20/mo or $200/yr; Max $200/mo or $2,000/yr. The official page reportedly leads with annual billing (Pro $17/mo, Max $167/mo). Enterprise Pro is $40/user/mo, or $34 billed annually ($400/seat/yr) — [aiviewer](https://aiviewer.ai/guides/ai-pricing-comparison-2026/); [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/perplexity-pricing); [insearch](https://insearch.ai/perplexity-enterprise-pricing)

**Mistral Le Chat (ranked 11th)**
- Pro $14.99/mo ($5.99 for verified students). Team $24.99/user/mo excl. tax. Enterprise on custom pricing — [aiviewer](https://aiviewer.ai/guides/ai-pricing-comparison-2026/); [nhimg](https://nhimg.org/enterprise-ai-assistant-plans-compared)

### Inferences
- A paid plan for a household is still largely unserved: only Google shares AI with up to 5 family members, and the two AI-first leaders (OpenAI, Anthropic) have no family plan.
- Splitting team seats into "Standard vs Premium (about 5x)" is now the pattern at both OpenAI and Anthropic, at near-identical prices ($25/$125 monthly, $20/$100 annual).
- Rolling-window limits (5 h + weekly) are mainly Anthropic's mechanism. OpenAI and Google describe limits as multiples of another tier, and OpenAI has dropped the text cap on its free tier.

### Gaps
- No official pricing page was fetched for any vendor; everything is from aggregators and press.
- Qwen consumer pricing, Gemini's exact free-tier daily caps, DeepSeek's consumer pricing as stated by DeepSeek, Grok and Perplexity family plans (none found), Meta One's final launch prices, and regional pricing (for example ChatGPT Go in India) were not established.

## Q3. For each product: mobile navigation pattern and appearance settings

### Takeaway
The two leaders moved to a **bottom tab bar** in the week of 19-20 Nov 2026. ChatGPT now has Home, Dots and Explore plus pinned features. Claude now has Chat, Projects, Code and Artifacts, with the hamburger menu kept for chat history. Gemini still uses a hamburger drawer with chat search. On appearance, the norm is Light / Dark / System. ChatGPT alone also offers accent colours and contrast, Claude offers no accent colour, and Gemini on Android follows the system theme with a true-black dark mode.

### Cited Findings
- **ChatGPT navigation:** redesign announced 19 Nov 2026, with a bottom navigation bar of Home, Dots and Explore. Users can pin other features to the bar. The rollout starts with Pro users who have, or are eligible for, a "Dot" — [Neoteo](https://www.neoteo.com/en/openai-announced-a-redesigned-chatgpt-mobile-experience-with-a-dedicated-dots-tab-starting-with-eligible-pro-users); [Lapaas Voice](https://lapaasvoice.com/chatgpt-mobile-app-gets-dedicated-dots-tab-and-redesigned-navigation)
- **ChatGPT appearance:** Settings > Appearance offers light, dark or system, plus contrast and an accent colour. Third-party guides list blue, green, yellow, pink, orange and purple, plus a neutral option (Black in light mode, White in dark). Availability varies by platform, plan and rollout — [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11958281-updating-your-visual-experience-on-chatgpt); [ChatTheme](https://chattheme.vercel.app/guides/how-to-change-chatgpt-theme)
- **Claude navigation:** rolling out from 20 Nov 2026 on iOS and Android, a bottom tab bar of Chat, Projects, Code and Artifacts. The hamburger menu stays, mainly for chat history — [dev.ua](https://dev.ua/en/news/anthropic-onovyla-dyzain-mobilnoho-zastosunku-claude-1795249677); [IntoMobile, 24 Nov 2026](https://www.intomobile.com/2026/11/24/claudes-android-app-finally-looks-like-it-belongs-on-android/); [Emergent](https://emergent.sh/news/claude-mobile-app-gets-new-tab-bar-code)
- **Claude appearance:** Light, Match System or Dark, with no accent, background or theme options beyond that (third-party guide) — [ChatTheme](https://chattheme.vercel.app/guides/how-to-change-claude-theme)
- **Gemini navigation:** the top-left chat icon became a hamburger that opens a drawer with "Search for chats" (Android and web, Feb 2026; iOS lagging). The Android homepage redesign added a "My Stuff" view **[possibly outdated]** — [Gadget Hacks](https://android.gadgethacks.com/news/google-gemini-android-app-gets-major-ui-overhaul/); [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/ai/google-tweaks-geminis-android-app-with-a-fresh-homepage-and-my-stuff-view); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/11/14/gemini-android-homepage-redesign/)
- **Gemini appearance:** on Android the dark mode depends entirely on the phone's system setting. An in-app light/dark override was spotted in development (24 Sep 2026) but has not shipped. Android dark mode moved to true black, though at the time the prompt box and conversation colours had not changed — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/gemini-dark-mode-3715309); [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-gemini-android-true-black-theme-3611192); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2024/11/07/gemini-dark-theme-web/)
- **Perplexity:** the iPad app was rebuilt (late Apr 2026) with desktop-style navigation and a persistent sidebar. The Comet browser on iPhone got "cleaner bottom controls" in May 2026 — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/perplexitys-new-ipad-app-adds-multitasking-and-research-tools-for-power-users-528049-2026-04-29); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/21/perplexitys-comet-ai-browser-for-ios-upgraded-with-8-major-improvements/); [The Tech Outlook](https://www.thetechoutlook.com/news/apps/perplexity-ios-update-brings-new-navigation-voice-enhancements-and-improved-search)

### Inferences
- On phones, the leading pattern is now a bottom tab bar for the product's main surfaces, with a drawer kept only for chat history. A drawer alone, as in Gemini, is the older pattern.
- The minimum appearance offering is Light/Dark/System. An accent colour is a differentiator, and only ChatGPT offers one natively.

### Gaps
- No sourced 2026 navigation or appearance details were found for the mobile apps of Meta AI, Copilot, DeepSeek, Grok, Doubao, Qwen or Perplexity (iPhone). Their appearance settings were not researched.
- Both bottom-bar rollouts are less than a week old, so the tab sets may still change.
