# Agentic and connected features of the top consumer AI assistants (as of 24 Nov 2026)

Scope: ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity (incl. Comet), Meta AI, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral (Le Chat, now Mistral Vibe), Manus / Genspark. Six areas: (1) scheduled tasks and agents, (2) browser/computer-use agents and approvals, (3) agentic checkout, (4) connectors/MCP/apps-in-chat, (5) sharing and collaboration, (6) local and country-specific services.

Method note for the report writer: collected 24 Nov 2026 with web search. Direct fetches of the primary hosts **blog.google, help.openai.com, aitoolsreview.co.uk and explainx.ai failed** because DNS resolution is blocked in this environment, so most findings below come from search-result summaries of the listed pages. They were not read in full. Where the only source is a third-party blog it is marked **[secondary]**, and where sources conflict that is stated. Anything dated before mid-2026 is marked **[pre-mid-2026]**.

## Key question 1: what exists for each product and feature, how it works, its limits, and its launch and update dates

### Takeaway
By November 2026 every major assistant except DeepSeek has some agent mode. The products have converged on four things: a "Scheduled" sidebar hub for one-off, recurring, monitoring and trigger-based jobs (ChatGPT, Claude Cowork, Perplexity Computer); a cloud browser plus an optional extension that works in your own signed-in browser (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Manus, Comet); MCP as the connector and apps standard (OpenAI Apps SDK, Claude's directory of about 974 connectors); and, for buying, discovery in chat with checkout handled by the merchant. The biggest surprise of 2026 is that OpenAI pulled back from in-chat checkout (March 2026) and retired group chats (July 2026). Google is now the most aggressive on agentic payments (UCP / Universal Cart / AP2, and free Gemini Tasks from 17 Nov 2026).

#### (2) Browser and computer-use agents, and their approvals

- **ChatGPT agent**: it "pauses and asks for confirmation prior to taking sensitive steps such as completing a purchase". **Watch Mode** on sensitive sites requires the tab to be active so the user watches, which OpenAI likens to "hands on the wheel". A later OpenAI post describes it as requiring explicit confirmation of sensitive actions such as sending messages or making payments. **Takeover mode** lets the user enter credentials or payment details themselves. Monitors stop the agent when its behavior looks suspicious, and the model card reports it ignoring 99.5% of synthetic injected instructions **[pre-mid-2026; Nov 2025 post]** — [OpenAI: prompt injections](https://openai.com/index/prompt-injections/); [Decrypt, Jul 2025](https://decrypt.co/330714/openais-chatgpt-agent-launches-expanded-powers-elevated-risk)
- OpenAI says prompt injection is "unlikely to ever be fully solved" (reported around Atlas) **[pre-mid-2026]** — [Fortune, 23 Dec 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/12/23/openai-ai-browser-prompt-injections-cybersecurity-hackers/)
- **ChatGPT Atlas** (OpenAI's agent browser) **shut down on 9 Aug 2026**, per one source. This was not confirmed by an OpenAI page here — [Wikipedia: ChatGPT Atlas (search summary)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT_Atlas)
- **Claude**: guidance is to use a connector when one exists, Cowork's built-in browser when no sign-in is needed, and **Claude in Chrome** only when a task needs a signed-in session and no connector exists — [Claude Cowork Connectors, usecarly [secondary]](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/claude-cowork-connectors/); [claudeforoperators [secondary]](https://www.claudeforoperators.com/workflows/connecting-tools/)
- **Manus Browser Operator** (Chrome/Edge extension) acts in the user's own signed-in browser, with permission per task. A security review of v0.0.63 says Manus's **server sends the clicks and typing**, and that while a task runs, **that tab's text, screenshots and files go to Manus** — [tooltivity [secondary]](https://tooltivity.com/extensions/manus-ai); [Taskade [secondary]](https://www.taskade.com/blog/manus-ai-review)
- **Gemini Tasks** run in isolated cloud VMs. Gemini asks for confirmation before the Buy step and offers "Always allow" for routine actions — [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/497176/google-gemini-tasks-india.html); [Google blog snippet](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/)
- **Perplexity Comet**: Amazon sued over Comet's agentic shopping. The **Ninth Circuit vacated the preliminary injunction on 4 Aug 2026** — [sanbi.ai Q4 2026 tracker [secondary]](https://sanbi.ai/blog/agentic-commerce-tracker-ai-checkout); [secnews [secondary]](https://www.secnews.gr/en/725171/paypal-perplexity-instant-buy-agentic-commerce-2026/)

#### (3) Agentic purchasing and checkout

- **OpenAI Instant Checkout + Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP)**: OpenAI and Stripe launched both on **29 Sep 2025**. ACP is an open spec co-developed with Stripe **[pre-mid-2026]** — [OpenAI: Buy it in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/)
- **On 24 Mar 2026 OpenAI changed course.** It said the first version of Instant Checkout "did not offer the level of flexibility that we aspire to provide", and moved to **discovery in ChatGPT with checkout on the merchant side**, through dedicated retailer apps (Walmart, Target, Instacart). Only about a dozen Shopify merchants had gone live. Sources describe the change as either "retired" or "moving to Apps" — [Hypotenuse [secondary]](https://www.hypotenuse.ai/blog/chatgpts-instant-checkout-the-next-phase-of-agentic-commerce); [Digital Applied [secondary]](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agentic-commerce-discover-in-ai-buy-on-site-2026); [Wikipedia: Agentic commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agentic_commerce). The ACP spec still exists — [Medusa docs](https://docs.medusajs.com/resources/how-to-tutorials/tutorials/agentic-commerce)
- **Google**: Universal Cart was previewed at I/O on **19–20 May 2026**. It is one cart across merchants that you can add to from Search, Gemini, YouTube and Gmail — [Digital Commerce 360, 20 May 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/05/20/google-universal-cart-for-agentic-commerce/); [Google Shopping blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/google-shopping-cart/). The **US launch was reported for 15 Oct 2026** in Search (AI Mode) and the Gemini app, with **price-drop and restock alerts** and **Google Pay** checkout, and PayPal "coming". The date was not confirmed on Google's own post — [explainx [secondary]](https://explainx.ai/blog/google-universal-cart-launch-ai-mode-gemini-us-2026); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-expands-universal-commerce-protocol-and-launches-new-agentic-shopping-tools-478113)
- **Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)** is Google's open standard for checkout. It is limited to eligible US retailers and listings, and Tapestry (Coach, Kate Spade) is one integrated retailer. **AP2's delegated "human-not-present" buying is described but was not part of the cart launch.** Indian brands cannot use UCP checkout yet — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-expands-universal-commerce-protocol-and-launches-new-agentic-shopping-tools-478113); [genrise [secondary]](https://genrise.ai/insights/universal-cart-agentic-commerce-2026); [Think with Google](https://business.google.com/us/think/ai-excellence/agentic-commerce-ai-tools-protocol-retailers-platforms/)
- **Gemini Tasks payments (17 Nov 2026)**: purchases go through **AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol)** using **Google Pay** with **Mastercard and Visa digital (agent) tokens**, with American Express "coming soon". Controls are **optional spending caps**, **confirmation before Buy**, and **"Always allow"** for routine actions. Purchases earn normal card rewards — [Google blog snippet](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/); [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/497176/google-gemini-tasks-india.html); [AskSurf [secondary]](https://asksurf.ai/pulse/en/google-gemini-tasks-agentic-commerce-expansion)
- **Microsoft Copilot Checkout**: announced **8 Jan 2026** and live in the US with **PayPal, Shopify, Stripe and Etsy**. PayPal provides inventory and branded/guest checkout including cards. Stripe passes transaction context to the merchant **over ACP**. Shopify merchants are **auto-enrolled after an opt-out window**. Microsoft takes **no commission**, and the merchant stays merchant of record. It is planned to expand to Bing, MSN and Edge, a later update added **mobile checkout and 500,000+ merchants**, and Brand Agents launched alongside. Reporters noted that the **error-prevention details (wrong product or price) were unclear** — [Axios, 8 Jan 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/08/microsoft-ai-copilot-checkout); [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-launches-copilot-checkout-joining-the-ai-shopping-race-against-amazon-google-and-openai/); [Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/etsy-shopify-among-early-partners-for-microsofts-copilot-checkout/156346/); [Windows Central (mobile, 500k merchants)](https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft/windows-11/copilots-shopping-upgrade-brings-checkout-to-the-mobile-app-with-deeper-data-from-half-a-million-merchants); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/microsoft-copilot-checkout-shopping/)
- **Perplexity Instant Buy with PayPal**: launched **25 Nov 2025** for US users and works with any merchant that accepts PayPal. The retailer is merchant of record. It is free for all US users except Enterprise plans, and limited to US adults with a PayPal account. **No 2026 primary update was found** **[pre-mid-2026]** — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/paypal-perplexity-instant-buy-shopping/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/ai/perplexity-announces-its-own-take-on-an-ai-shopping-assistant-210500961.html); [Perplexity changelog](https://www.perplexity.ai/changelog/what-we-shipped---december-5th); [sanbi.ai tracker [secondary]](https://sanbi.ai/blog/agentic-commerce-tracker-ai-checkout)

#### (5) Sharing and collaboration

- **ChatGPT group chats are being retired.** From **9 Jul 2026**, users on web, iOS and Android can no longer create group chats, convert conversations into them, or join through invite links. Existing group chats stay usable "for now" — [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)
- **ChatGPT shared projects** are available on Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu, on web and mobile. Members pool chats, files and instructions, and in a shared ChatGPT project **everyone can read everyone's chats** — [Help: Projects in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt); [smithstephen [secondary]](https://www.smithstephen.com/p/in-a-shared-chatgpt-project-everyone); [OpenAI: More ways to work with your team](https://openai.com/index/more-ways-to-work-with-your-team/)
- **ChatGPT scheduled tasks can be shared** (Free/Go/Plus/Pro, Aug 2026). On Business plans, teammates can review task results and edit instructions together (Sep 2026) — [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes); [Business release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11391654-chatgpt-business-release-notes)
- **Claude Projects** can be shared with members of your organization on Team/Enterprise, at **view** level (see and chat, no edits) or **edit** level. **Chats inside a project are not shared**. Sharing with a group is Enterprise-only and in beta. An individual chat can be shared as a **read-only snapshot** — [Claude Help: Manage project visibility and sharing](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519189-manage-project-visibility-and-sharing); [Claude Help: What are projects](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9517075-what-are-projects); [display.dev [secondary]](https://display.dev/blog/share-claude-chatgpt-project-with-team)
- **Claude Tag**, a handoff in Slack, was reported as shipping 23 Jun 2026 in beta for Team/Enterprise. **Not confirmed on an Anthropic page** — [eesel [secondary]](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/claude-ai-collaboration-tools)
- **Copilot Groups** supports up to **32 participants** (fall 2025) **[pre-mid-2026; current status unverified]** — [WindowsForum, Fall Release](https://windowsforum.com/threads/microsoft-copilot-fall-release-groups-connectors-memory-and-edge-actions.386101/)
- **Whether the recipient's continued work syncs back**: a shared ChatGPT project is live and pooled. A shared Claude chat is a snapshot, so the recipient's continuation does not sync back. **No source found** covers handing a conversation to another person with two-way sync, or live co-editing inside a chat, for any product.

### Inferences
- The industry's 2026 pattern for scheduled work is a **single "Scheduled" hub** (list of upcoming runs, pause/resume/edit/delete) with **three trigger kinds**: time, change-monitoring that notifies only on change, and event webhooks (new mail, Slack message, PR). ChatGPT has the most complete version. Claude Cowork matches it on cloud runs, and Perplexity meters each run with credits.
- OpenAI's retreat on in-chat checkout (Mar 2026), together with Copilot's "merchant of record, no commission" model, suggests that **owning checkout inside the assistant has been hard to make work**. The surviving designs keep the merchant as merchant of record and use tokenised network credentials (AP2 / Visa and Mastercard agent tokens) rather than the assistant holding card numbers.
- Group chat is not a settled category. OpenAI retiring group chats in July 2026 suggests low use or high moderation cost. Durable collaboration happens at the **project** level, and privacy models differ: ChatGPT pools chats, Claude keeps them private.
- Local and booking features are thin outside the US. Only Google publishes multi-country availability, which leaves room for an assistant that can show it really works in each country.

## Key question 2: which products are best-in-class in each area, and what safety and approval patterns they use for actions that spend money or change accounts

### Takeaway
Best-in-class as of Nov 2026, based on the sourced evidence:
- **Scheduled tasks:** ChatGPT.
- **Agentic payments:** Google (Gemini Tasks + AP2 with network tokens, spending caps and confirm-before-Buy).
- **Connector breadth:** Claude (about 974 MCP connectors, with interactive in-chat apps).
- **Enterprise write governance:** Microsoft ("Needs approval" by default plus an admin review window).
- **Project sharing:** ChatGPT, with Claude's private-by-default model as the privacy-first alternative.
- **Local booking reach:** Google.

The approval patterns that recur: a confirmation before the final irreversible step, the user physically taking over for credentials and payment, a watch requirement on sensitive sites, standing "always allow" grants scoped to a routine or a schedule, optional spending caps, and the merchant remaining merchant of record.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The strongest money pattern found combines five controls: tokenised credentials the agent never sees in plain form (AP2 / network tokens), an optional per-user spending cap, explicit confirmation of the exact cart before Buy, "always allow" grants limited to a named routine, and the merchant as merchant of record so refunds and disputes run on existing rails. No single source documents a product that **reads back** the order after purchase to verify it. That would be a differentiator, but it is unconfirmed whether any competitor does it.
- For scheduled jobs, approval granted per task (Claude) is safer than approval granted per connector globally. Notifying only on change (ChatGPT monitoring tasks) is the delivery pattern users seem to want.
- For writes to accounts, Microsoft's "new write tool starts as Needs approval" is the clearest least-privilege default seen among the competitors.

### Gaps
- No source found shows exact default spending-cap values, or whether caps are per transaction or per month, for Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity.
- No source documents refunds or undo for agent purchases, or a post-purchase verification step, in any competitor.
- How Claude in Chrome and Gemini Tasks handle confirmations for account changes such as sending email or deleting data was not found in a primary 2026 source.
- No 2026 primary source on whether OpenAI's Watch Mode and takeover mode carried over into ChatGPT Work after Atlas closed.
